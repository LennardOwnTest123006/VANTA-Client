Small update of VANTA Launcher 1.4.0. The Logs page shows its times in 12-hour time with AM and PM, like the VANTA Client 1.4.1 clock. Nothing else changed: installing, PLAY, the Local AI and the updater work as in 1.4.0.

## Improved

- **Logs page times in AM/PM**: each line starts with a time such as `2:05:09 PM` instead of `14:05:09`

## Notes

- Launchers from 1.0.1 on see the update by themselves and offer the file that matches how they were installed; on Windows that is `VANTA-Launcher-1.4.1.msi`
